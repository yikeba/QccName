QCC · Enterprise Namespace

企业账户体系的
抗量子迁移Post-quantum migration
for enterprise accounts

RSA 和 ECDSA 撑起了今天几乎所有的企业账户、登录和签名。量子计算成熟后，这些签名可以被伪造。企业级 Namespace 让企业不必重建账本，就能把账户体系先换到 ML-DSA-87 抗量子身份上：关键管理名由企业签名上链、随时换人；员工与客户账户照常注册；谁能进入企业系统，由企业自己的白名单决定。RSA and ECDSA underpin nearly every enterprise account, login and signature today. Once quantum computing matures, those signatures can be forged. An enterprise Namespace lets a company move its account system to ML-DSA-87 post-quantum identities without rebuilding its ledger: management names are signed on-chain by the company and reassigned at will; staff and customer accounts register as usual; who may enter company systems is decided by the company's own whitelists.

企业为什么现在就要准备Why enterprises should prepare now

量子迁移不是换一个算法库，而是换掉整个账户体系的信任根。越早有一条不停机的迁移路径，越从容。Quantum migration is not swapping a crypto library — it replaces the root of trust of the whole account system. The earlier a no-downtime path exists, the calmer the transition.

签名可被伪造Signatures become forgeable

RSA / ECDSA 依赖的数学难题会被量子算法破解。到那时，登录凭证、交易签名、管理员授权都可能被冒充。The hard problems behind RSA / ECDSA fall to quantum algorithms. Logins, transaction signatures and admin approvals can then be impersonated.

核心账本不能停The core ledger cannot stop

银行、贸易、学校的账本运行了十几年，重建风险极高。迁移必须在旧系统旁边进行，而不是推倒重来。Ledgers at banks, traders and schools have run for decades; rebuilding is too risky. Migration has to happen beside the old system, not instead of it.

管理身份要能随时换人Management identities must change hands

管理员离职、岗位调整每天都在发生。管理身份必须由企业单方面控制，不能依赖个人配合交出权限。Admins leave and roles change every day. Management identities must be controlled by the company alone, never dependent on an individual handing them back.

公开 Namespace 与企业级 NamespacePublic vs enterprise Namespace

两者用同一套协议和节点程序，只是 tag 的定位不同。游戏、公开邮箱、IM 适合公开 Namespace；银行、外贸公司、学校适合企业级 Namespace。Both run the same protocol and node software; only the role of the tag differs. Games, public mail and IM fit a public Namespace; banks, trading companies and schools fit an enterprise one.

三层安全模型A three-layer security model

底线很简单：就算任何人都注册了一个 .osn 名字，企业也不受影响。因为名字只证明「这个名字对应这个地址」，能不能进入企业系统，是另外两层说了算。The baseline is simple: even if anyone registers a .osn name, the company is unaffected. A name only proves "this name maps to this address"; entering company systems is decided by the other two layers.

1 · Namespace + Reserved

管理层立足Management stands on solid ground

  • admin、cto、audit 等管理名由企业签名上链，别人注册不了，形近名也不行admin, cto, audit are signed on-chain by the company; nobody else can register them or lookalikes
  • 换人：发布新一版，全网在同一区块高度切换，不需要离职者配合Reassign: publish a new version; the whole network switches at one block height, no cooperation needed
  • 冻结：把可疑名字写入 Reserved 空地址，原注册立即移除Freeze: list a suspicious name with an empty address; its registration is removed at once
2 · 应用白名单App whitelists

注册了也进不来Registered is not admitted

  • 企业邮箱、内部登录、账户账本，各自维护钱包地址白名单Company mail, internal login and the account ledger each keep their own address whitelist
  • 白名单热加载：开通、移除即时生效，移出的地址会话立即作废Hot-reloaded: grants and removals apply at once; removed addresses lose their sessions
  • 对外可提供核验接口：「abc.osn 是否为本公司账户」Optionally expose a check: "is abc.osn one of our accounts?"
3 · 部署Deployment

自己的节点，自己的边界Your nodes, your boundary

  • 企业自建节点集群，与公开 Namespace 完全隔离，可与之同机部署The company runs its own cluster, fully isolated from the public Namespace, even on the same servers
  • 查询接口可放公网，也可只放内网或加访问控制Query APIs can be public, private-only, or access-controlled
  • 协议 hash 锚定在 QCC 链上，任何节点都无法私改规则The protocol hash is anchored on QCC; no node can quietly change the rules
+ · 发布地址Publisher address

安全底线单独管理The last line, managed separately

  • 只有发布引导协议的那个地址能发布 ReservedOnly the address that published the protocol may publish Reserved
  • 这个地址的保护（多签、冷钱包、审批流程）属于链上账户安全，与 Namespace 解耦Protecting it (multi-sig, cold storage, approvals) is chain-account security, decoupled from Namespace

核心优势Key advantages

不改账本，先换身份New identity, same ledger

现有账户号映射到 Namespace 名字，旧系统照常运行，身份层先完成抗量子。Existing account numbers map to Namespace names; the old system keeps running while the identity layer goes post-quantum.

ML-DSA-87 抗量子ML-DSA-87 post-quantum

NIST 标准化的最高安全等级签名算法，2592 字节公钥。The highest security level of the NIST-standardized signature scheme, with 2592-byte public keys.

没有密码可泄露No passwords to leak

登录是钱包签名，服务器不存密码，撞库、钓鱼密码失去意义。Login is a wallet signature; servers store no passwords, so credential stuffing and password phishing lose their point.

管理权单方面可控Unilateral control of management

管理名换人、冻结可疑账户，只需企业发布一版 Reserved。Reassigning management names or freezing suspicious accounts takes one Reserved version from the company.

全程可审计Fully auditable

每一版的发布地址、区块高度、内容 hash 都在链上，「某天谁是管理员」可以回溯。Publisher, block height and content hash of every version are on-chain; "who was admin on that day" is answerable.

一个账户，多个系统One account, many systems

邮箱、内部系统、账本共用一个 Namespace 身份，各自用白名单控制权限。Mail, internal systems and the ledger share one Namespace identity, each controlling access with its own whitelist.

行业场景Use cases

以下银行、外贸、学校均为方案示例；OSN 是已经上线的实际部署。The bank, trading and school cases are illustrative designs; OSN is a live deployment.

商业银行：四步完成抗量子迁移Commercial bank: post-quantum in four steps

  1. 影子绑定Shadow binding
    管理层名字进 Reserved；客户账户号映射为 Namespace 名字，绑定银行内部托管生成的抗量子地址。旧登录照常。Management names go into Reserved; customer account numbers map to Namespace names bound to post-quantum addresses generated in the bank's custody. Old logins continue.
  2. 双签Dual signing
    大额转账、改绑、管理操作同时要求旧签名和 Namespace 签名。Large transfers, rebinding and admin actions require both the old signature and a Namespace signature.
  3. 以抗量子为主Post-quantum first
    Namespace 签名为准，旧签名降为兼容。Namespace signatures become authoritative; old ones are kept for compatibility.
  4. 下线旧算法Retire the old algorithms
    关闭 RSA / ECDSA，迁移完成。Switch off RSA / ECDSA; migration complete.

外贸公司Trading company

员工分布在多个国家，无法依赖内网。员工自己注册名字，企业邮箱和文件系统用白名单开通；业务员离职，从白名单移除即可，客户往来邮件留在企业邮箱。Staff span several countries, so a private network is impractical. Staff register their own names; mail and file systems grant access by whitelist. When a salesperson leaves, remove them from the whitelist — customer correspondence stays in the company mailbox.

学校School

教务、财务、招生等岗位名进 Reserved；学生账户由学校应用协助注册，学籍系统白名单控制。毕业后从白名单移除，名字可以继续作为校友身份使用。Registrar, finance and admissions roles go into Reserved; student accounts are registered with help from the school app and gated by the records-system whitelist. After graduation, removal from the whitelist leaves the name usable as an alumni identity.

企业接入流程How a company gets started

拥有企业 tagOwn a company tag

在 QCC 公链发起 tag 规则交易，例如 .bank、.trade，永久归属、可以转让。Send a tag-rule transaction on QCC, e.g. .bank or .trade; owned permanently and transferable.

发布引导协议Publish the bootstrap protocol

用企业发布地址（建议多签）把协议文件上链。这个地址从此是唯一能发布 Reserved 的地址。Anchor the protocol file on-chain from the company publisher address (multi-sig recommended). From then on it is the only address that can publish Reserved.

部署节点Run nodes

3 台起步即可容忍 1 台故障；可与公开 Namespace 同机运行，数据完全隔离。Three nodes tolerate one failure; they can share servers with a public Namespace while keeping data fully separate.

发布 ReservedPublish Reserved

列出管理名与对应地址，签名上链，交易 hash 写入节点的 Reserved.config，30 秒内全网生效。List management names and addresses, sign them on-chain, and add the transaction hash to the nodes' Reserved.config — live network-wide within 30 seconds.

接入应用白名单Connect apps with whitelists

企业邮箱、内部系统通过 qccauth 完成签名登录，各自配置白名单，开通谁、移除谁由企业决定。Company mail and internal systems use qccauth for signature login, each with its own whitelist — the company decides who is in or out.
